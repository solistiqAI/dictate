What Dictate does, how, and how each part was checked. The short version is in the README; the
measurements behind the model choice are in `docs/architecture.md`.

| "Korrigieren" (`src/correct.rs`, a switch per shortcut in window → Kurzbefehle next to Formatieren, `correct = true` on a `[[trigger]]`, before Formatieren when both are on): filler words ("äh", "ähm") go by rule; where the text holds a correction cue ("nein", "ah ne", "sorry", "ich meine", "Moment", "das ist falsch", …) a language model proposes the text without the self-correction ("um drei, äh nein, um fünf Uhr" → "um fünf Uhr"). The model is Gemma 4 E4B (lmstudio-community's Q4_K_M GGUF, which keeps the embeddings at Q6_K: unsloth's Q4_K_M missed misheard corrections Ollama's build fixes, `src/llm.rs`, llama.cpp via `llama-cpp-2` on the GPU with Metal kernels embedded as source; loaded in the background once a shortcut corrects and kept loaded, system prompt cached in the KV cache), chosen in window → Erkennung → Korrigieren (`correct_model`, "Laden" fetches 5 GB) or `dictate model download gemma-4-e4b`; without it, or when it fails, Apple's on-device model (`dictate-ane correct`, same instructions, `src/correct-prompt.txt`). Gemma chosen but not downloaded while a shortcut tidies: a banner on Kurzbefehle and Erkennung ("Gemma laden"), the overlay says it once per start after a paste, `install.sh` offers the download (`DICTATE_GEMMA=1`/`0`), and a Gemma downloaded from the command line is taken at the next recording, no restart. With Gemma every dictation is asked (no cue check: Parakeet hears "äh nein" as "hinein", "eine", "Arne", "Anni"; the prompt names these), long ones cut at sentence ends into pieces of about 40 words that run side by side in one batch, so the wait hardly grows with the length (203 real dictations: median 0.51 s, max 1.31 s at 180 words). While recording, each finished sentence of the live text (unchanged over two passes) goes to Gemma once in the background, and the overlay shows the words it cuts dimmed and struck through; the pass after releasing reuses those proposals when it gets the same text (a 60 s dictation: 14 sentences, about 5 s of GPU time, 1.2 CPU-s). Each cut is checked on its own: refused ones go back, the others stay. Gemma's proposals only have to be deletions (in order), at most 12 retracted words per cut (cue words not counted) and half of a piece, must not drop a cue alone, and a whole sentence without a cue goes back unless the next cut sentence starts with a strong cue ("Dienstag. Nein, das ist falsch …"); each live sentence logs a "live correct:" line; Apple keeps the stricter check below. Only deletions are taken: the proposal must be the transcript's words in order, each cut must end in a cue with a pause before it (with Gemma also one word after a pause that sounds like "ne"/"nein": -ne, -nee, -nein, -nen, -nö, at most 6 letters) and keep the words after it, at most 12 retracted words per cue and half the text; anything else (answered questions, written mails, reordered or renumbered words, the wrong half kept) leaves the transcript and the log says why. Kept words keep Parakeet's spelling. Text without a cue never reaches the model. Measured on the 25 sentences of `eval/corrections.tsv` (43 sentences: a benchmark set, misheard cues, dictations) through the whole pipeline, `dictate correct --eval eval/corrections.tsv [--model apple]`: Gemma 24/26 corrected, 18/19 left alone, 0.23 s median per model call, load 0.4 s warm (2.8 s cold), 0.55 GB memory footprint (weights mapped from the file); Apple 9/26, 19/19. ## First start

First start: macOS asks for **Accessibility** (to see the trigger in other apps and to paste) and
**Microphone**. Dictate.app gets its own entries; `bundle.sh` signs with `$DICTATE_SIGN_IDENTITY` (or a brand's identity) so they survive rebuilds; ad-hoc signing loses them on every build. Run from a terminal, the permissions belong to
the terminal app, and Terminal.app cannot get the microphone at all (use Ghostty/iTerm or the app).
Without a terminal the log goes to `~/Library/Application Support/dictate/dictate.log`.

Only "Thank you." or "(nothing heard)"? The microphone delivers silence, almost always because the
terminal app has no Microphone permission. `dictate mic` records 3 seconds and prints the level;
`dictate` itself checks at startup and keeps the last recording in
`~/Library/Application Support/dictate/last.wav` (`dictate transcribe` it to compare).

Mouse buttons: Logi Options+ swallows the MX Master buttons. Either give the button a key combo in
Options+ and use that combo as the trigger (`-t ctrl+alt+cmd+d`), or set the button back to its
plain mouse function and use `-t mouse3` / `-t mouse4`.

Replacements: copy `replacements.example.txt` to
`~/Library/Application Support/dictate/replacements.txt`.
