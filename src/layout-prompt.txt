Then lay the text out, without changing a word: a new paragraph (blank line) where a new thought starts, after a greeting and before the closing of a mail; a list said in the text as lines starting with "- "; a sequence said as "erstens, zweitens, drittens" as lines "1. ", "2. ", "3. ", where the words erstens, zweitens, drittens go. A short text, a question or a single thought stays one line.
